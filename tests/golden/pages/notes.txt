                      Click a rail label to open that page -- or press c-n / c-p to step through the         ░
                      families, the ends wrap. tab moves the page's focus ring, the arrows and enter drive   ░
                      whatever it focuses, space marks the focused view's cursor row, c-o walks the focus    ░
                      into the page's own right pane (the shell page opens one), c-f opens the search tier   ░
                      over the page, c-g mounts the developer stats box, and q quits.                        ░
                                                                                                             ░
                      The mouse works the same way: moving the pointer raises a hover band and the hovered   ░
                      leaf's own hint in the tooltip line under it; a press and a release on one cell is a   ░
                      click; the wheel scrolls the pane under the pointer, not the focused one. A drag across░
                      any rows selects them, and the release copies the range through the terminal's OSC 52  ░
                      -- a toast says how many lines went to the clipboard.                                  ░
