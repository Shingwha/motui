# ADR-009 · 2.0 is intentionally incompatible

- **Status:** Accepted (2.0)
- **Context:** The 2.0 work renamed or replaced a large part of the call
  surface: `render` takes constraints, blocks became sections, the driver
  split into runtime/workspace/terminal, one-shot results changed their
  name, and the positional direction strings became enums.
- **Decision:** Ship a break. Do not keep shims or aliases for the removed
  names; provide a migration table, recipes and executable examples
  instead.
- **Why:** A clean break avoids two doors for every concept and the long
  tail of compatibility debt. The project is still young enough that the
  migration is a bounded, documented edit.
- **Consequences:** Users must migrate deliberately. `MIGRATION.md` is a
  first-class deliverable. Internal names may survive where they are still
  the implementation's honest vocabulary, but they are not part of the
  public facade.
- **Alternatives:** A compatibility layer (rejected: doubles the test
  surface and hides the new model); a gradual deprecation cycle over
  several minors (rejected: the breadth of the rename makes it impractical).
