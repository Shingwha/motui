# motui 1.1.8 — the surface taxonomy

**One token per role, one rule per row.** The 1.1.8 wave restructures three
families so each carries exactly one concept, and every choice is anchored in
what the reference toolkits do — Textual's design tokens (`$surface`,
`$block-cursor-background`, `$input-cursor-background`), Rich's
`Padding(style=…)`, urwid's `AttrMap` — verified against their documentation
while the wave was designed. Nothing here is a knob bolted onto the side: the
release deletes more than it adds.

**The band vocabulary is one token per role.** `Theme` gains `ground` (the
resting surface a row or region sits on, `NONE` by default),
`ground_active` (the engaged row's surface — the keyboard cursor row and the
pointer-hovered row share it today, and Textual's cursor/hover split is the
recorded growth path) and `caret` (the text caret's own block, split out of
the old `cursor`). The old `cursor` becomes `focus`: it names the band a
focused *control* paints, not the caret and not a row. And `selection` goes
back to being only what its name says — text selection — after `List`,
`Tree` and `Table` had all been borrowing it for their cursor rows.

**The row-sizing family is three members, one job each.** `fit(width)` trims,
`fill(width, style)` extends and surfaces — the style merges *underneath* the
content segments, the Rich `Padding(style=…)` rule, and it never truncates —
and `filled(width, style)` builds a blank row. `Line.pad_to` is gone: `fill`'s
`NONE` fast path answers byte-for-byte what it did, and the three places that
hand-rolled a band (`Panel`'s borderless mode, the search overlay, the toast
stack) now compose through the one primitive instead of each keeping a private
copy of the synthesis.

**The row-band contract is one rule for the whole family.** Every vertical
row-carrying widget — `List`, `Tree`, `Table`, `NavList`, `Select` and its
presets — resolves its bands once, at construction, through the charter: the
resting surface is `ground_style=` or the theme's `ground`, the engaged band
is `ground_active`, an unfocused engaged row merges `dim`, and a row is its
text-styled segments then `fit(width).fill(width, band)`. The band spans the
painting width, so a host that themes `ground` gets a real surface across the
pane instead of per-row patches; while the band is `NONE` the fill is skipped
and resting rows render exactly what they always did. Hosts that wrap a
component forward nothing by hand any more: `Delegate` is the single-child
identity wrapper — `render`, `revision`, `click` and `hover_at` forwarded,
`child_at` answered, the controller verbs never proxied — the urwid `AttrMap`
pattern over motui's protocols, with the three degradation rules pinned by
tests.

**The layout structs know their own geometry.** `ScreenLayout` carries the
`width`/`height` it was laid out in — the viewport's and the frame's composed
alike — and `Pane.inner_rect` joins `Pane.band(chrome)` as the frame's public
geometry reads, so a host anchors an IME cursor by reading the rectangle
instead of remembering the width beside a wrapper. `Markdown(max_width=…)`
caps the wrap width the way Textual's `max-width` does, beside its existing
`divider_max_width` sibling.

**Breaking — four documented clean cuts, no shim.** `Theme.cursor` is
`Theme.focus` (the constant `CURSOR` is `FOCUS`; `Radio`/`CheckGroup`'s
`focus_style` parameter is `selected_style`); `Line.pad_to` is removed; and
the engaged row of every widget in the contract now spans the painting width
at the default theme — the Textual/ratatui cursor-row norm, and the change
that makes a themed `ground` a surface rather than a patch. Resting rows are
unchanged byte for byte while the band is `NONE`. The library is young and
the cuts are documented here, so they ship under the year-let rule; the
deprecation cycle waits for a name with real users.

**Gates.** 1612 tests pass (42 new), the eleven golden pages match, both demo
paths exit 0, and the framebench render-hash is unmoved (`f872187a…`) — the
benchmark's workload composes no row widgets, so the default-visual change
lives in the styled bytes, where a 1,928-row A/B probe (every widget, every
state, base against head) confirms exactly the engaged rows moved and nothing
else. The golden dump is plain text and cannot see a style-only change; that
blind spot is recorded, with the probe as its evidence, for the next wave's
gate.
