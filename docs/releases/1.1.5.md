# motui 1.1.5 — the modal layer, the family merge, the rebuilt demo

**New — the blocking layer.** `Modal` scrims the composed frame, places its
panel centered or anchored at a cell, routes every event into the panel
body-first and dismisses on a click outside; `Dialog` is its pure component,
a title edge, a body and a `ButtonSpec` button row where `Enter` takes the
focused button, `Escape` cancels and a `danger=True` dialog opens on the last
non-dangerous button. `confirm_dialog` and `prompt_dialog` are the yes/no box
and the one-line prompt.

**New — components and features.** `ComboBox` (an editable `Input` over a
filtered dropdown, painted through a host-mounted `Modal`); `CheckGroup`,
`Radio` and `Autocomplete` as presets of one `Select` whose five knobs
(`wrap` / `commit` / `multi` / `marked` / `window`) are the whole variation
catalog; `Input(mask=)` / `Editor(mask=)`; `marked` + `toggle()` on `Table`
and `List` with the `[x]` row prefix; `NavList` for the rail, and `Tabs` as
the horizontal content strip with the indicator vocabulary (accent label +
underline, unfocused drops to `muted`); `CursorWindow` and `MarkSet` in the
kernel.

**Fixed — the families and the tiers.** The widget families live one file
each (`action.py`, `choice.py`, `textfield.py`, `rows.py`, `indicators.py`),
and the interaction tier absorbed `chrome.py` as
`interaction/{overlays,modal,palette,scrollbar}.py`. The root facade carries
34 names; `Dialog`, `Modal` and `NavList` joined it.

**The demo, rebuilt.** `python -m motui` runs eleven pages — overview, text,
layout, actions, forms, views, modals, shell, themes, streaming, notes — on a
grouped rail (foundations / components / shell). Every page rides one
template: its title rule, one factual line, then `── section ──` rules with
the live exhibit and at most one caption. The prose moved to the `notes`
manual: how to play, the tour, the signature quick reference and the
interaction model. Every component the library ships has a live exhibit.

**Breaking, by declaration.** This cycle's removals and incompatible changes
ship on the 1.1.x line and are declared in the CHANGELOG's `Breaking` note:
`Confirm` (replaced by `Dialog` / `confirm_dialog`), the top-level
`chrome.py`, thirteen merged or renamed widget modules,
`interaction/search.py` → `find.py`, the generated `docs/API.md` (and its
`tools/apidoc.py`), `Tabs`'s vertical mode and reverse-video default, and
`Palette`'s new `tui`-first constructor. No shims, no aliases. Since none of
these names had real users yet, the removals are clean cuts; from here the
MAJOR cadence is deliberately slow — about one a year — and the year's
documented breaking changes ride the MINOR/PATCH releases between.
